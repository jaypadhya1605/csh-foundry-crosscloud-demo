## What Murali Wants From This Call (and the Series)

### 1. **A unified, enterprise‑grade *agent orchestration platform***

Murali’s core ask is **not individual agents or demos**, but a **single, centralized platform** where multiple agents can be:

*   Triggered by events
*   Orchestrated together
*   Monitored and governed in one place

He explicitly said they **do not have a unified multi‑agent platform today** and that this is a **gap** for them.

> “We don’t have currently the multi‑agent orchestration platform… If we have a single pane of dashboard that says ‘these are all the agents and they’re talking to each other’ — that’s what I’m looking for.”

### 2. **Event‑driven, headless agent workflows (not UI demos)**

Murali repeatedly emphasized **automation over UI interaction**:

*   Agents triggered by **messages/events** (e.g., Pub/Sub, queues)
*   Headless execution (no human clicking)
*   End‑to‑end flows that **run on their own**

> “I get a message in the queue… I should be able to kick off an agent… make a decision and take an action.”

Later, he reinforced that **internal workflows don’t need chatbot‑style guardrails demos** as much as **operational automation**:

> “A lot of our workflows are strictly internal… It’s all about workflow and how it reduces overhead… Any headless agentic automation is what I’m interested in.”

### 3. **Cross‑cloud integration (especially GCP / BigQuery)**

A major requirement: **Foundry must work with their existing GCP data estate**.

He clearly described:

*   Data warehouse in **GCP / BigQuery**
*   Events originating there
*   Agents running elsewhere (e.g., Foundry)
*   Secure, governed access back to BigQuery

> “Most of our data warehouse is in GCP… If we want Microsoft Foundry as our agentic orchestration platform, these two have to be seamless.”

This is why **MCP tools + orchestration** resonated strongly for him.

What he wants:

*   Across **agents built on different frameworks**
*   Even when agents are **not hosted directly in Foundry**

This is why DSPM, platform‑agnostic observability, and “bring your own agent” messaging landed.
